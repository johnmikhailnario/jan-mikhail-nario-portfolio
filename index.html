The Voice in the Old Radio

Every night, seventeen-year-old Lucas passed the old radio that belonged to his grandfather. It had been sitting quietly in their house for years, and nobody used it anymore.

One evening, Lucas heard a familiar voice coming from the radio. He stopped and listened carefully. It sounded like his late grandfather.

The voice spoke about family, love, and the importance of taking care of the people who matter most. Lucas could hardly believe what he was hearing.

Before the radio went silent, he heard one final message: "Take care of her for me."

Lucas understood that the message was about his grandmother. From that night on, he made a promise to spend more time with her and help her whenever he could.

He never heard the voice again, but he kept the old radio. It reminded him that some messages arrive late—but never too late.

Story Reflection

The story teaches us to value our family and the people who care for us. It reminds us that even simple messages can have a deep meaning. We should show love and appreciation to our loved ones while we have the chance.

Light in the Darkness: Hope and Perseverance in "The Small Lamp"

The story "The Small Lamp" shows the importance of hope and perseverance in life. It teaches readers that even a small source of light can make a difference during difficult situations.

The main character, Daniel, experiences challenges that make his life difficult. Like many people, he faces moments when he feels uncertain about what will happen next. However, he continues to move forward despite his problems.

The small lamp in the story represents hope. Even though its light is not very strong, it helps Daniel see through the darkness. This shows that hope does not have to be big to give someone the courage to continue.

Darkness represents the challenges and problems that people experience. It reminds us that life is not always easy and that everyone faces difficult moments.

Daniel's experience teaches us to remain patient and strong. Instead of giving up when things become hard, we should learn from our experiences and continue doing our best.

The story also shows that simple things can give people comfort and encouragement. A small act of kindness, a word of support, or a little hope can help someone feel stronger.

Overall, "The Small Lamp" reminds readers that hope and perseverance are important in overcoming life's challenges. It teaches us to keep believing in ourselves even when the future seems unclear.

In conclusion, the story gives us a meaningful lesson: even a small light can make a difference in someone's life.

View Full Activity

Business Planning: Vision, Mission, and Goals

A business needs a clear vision and mission to guide its plans and actions. The vision describes what the business wants to achieve in the future, while the mission explains its purpose and what it wants to provide.

A business should also set SMART goals. These goals should be specific, measurable, achievable, relevant, and time-bound. Having clear goals helps the business owner know what steps to take.

One important lesson I learned is that earning money is not the only part of managing a business. A business owner should understand sales, expenses, capital, and profit.

The money earned from sales can help return the capital and provide profit. Some of the earnings can also be used as additional capital for the next batch of products.

This activity taught me that planning, proper management, and wise financial decisions are important in running a successful business.

Digital Citizenship and Online Safety

Digital citizenship means using technology and the internet in a responsible, respectful, and safe way. As students, we should understand that our actions online can affect ourselves and other people.

One important part of digital citizenship is protecting personal information. We should avoid sharing private details with strangers and use strong passwords to protect our accounts.

We should also think carefully before clicking links, downloading files, or sharing information. Not all information found online is true or safe.

Another important lesson is respecting other people online. We should avoid cyberbullying, use proper language, and treat others with kindness.

I learned that being a responsible digital citizen means making good decisions whenever we use the internet.

Truth, Opinion, and Critical Thinking

Truth and opinion are different. A fact or truth can be supported by reliable information and evidence, while an opinion is based on what a person thinks or believes.

In our daily lives, we often see different information on social media. Some information may be misleading or false, so we should not believe everything immediately.

We need to check the source, look for evidence, and understand the information before sharing it. This helps prevent the spread of misinformation.

Critical thinking is important because it helps us analyze situations, ask questions, and make better decisions.

Through this activity, I learned to be more careful with information and to use reason and evidence when forming my own conclusions.
